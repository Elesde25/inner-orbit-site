## 1. One-paragraph product definition

Inner Orbit is a **beautiful, affordable, content-rich, interconnected self-discovery universe** combining astrology, numerology, personal growth, interactive symbolic interpretation, expert-grade technical charts, reflection, personalized action plans, friendship, and an explorable personal galaxy. It is not another birth-chart calculator, conventional dashboard, static report, spiritual fear engine, or ordinary social network with star imagery. Users arrive through an unforgettable celestial transition, enter their personal **Celestial Soul Hub**, explore approachable yet substantive destinations, discover what resonates with their lived experiences, ask **Show Me Why** and **Show Me How**, pursue chosen growth paths, and share achievements and customizable universes with friends. The external cosmos is a mirror; **the extraordinary potential is within the person**.

**North-star lines (conceptually approved):**

- **“There Is More to You Than You've Discovered.”**
- **“Discover the extraordinary within.”**
- **“The universe may be the mirror, but the magic has always been within you.”**
- **“Discover. Understand. Become.”**
- **“Written in the stars. Lived by you.”**
- **“A world where becoming your best self helps others become theirs.”**

Use one or two where appropriate, not every line on every screen. Brand copy must sound inspiring, personal, elegant, and grounded rather than vague, preachy, fatalistic, or manipulative.

## 2. The founder's non-negotiable vision

1. **Wonder:** The first impression should feel like a premium cinematic game, crafted with exceptional art direction. Make people think “I have to show someone this.” The site should **feel like a living universe**, not stacked webpage sections decorated with cosmic wallpapers.
2. **Substance from the first public release:** Breadth is a primary value proposition. Astrology *and* numerology and personal development and other coherent pathways are integrated under one roof. People should sense many meaningful places to explore without being dumped into an overwhelming feature list. Do not launch a visually attractive, essentially empty demo.
3. **Clarity:** Beginners see readable synthesis and inviting interactions by default, not dense jargon. Advanced, traditional technical charts and controls remain accessible in **Discover / Explore / Expert** modes, with the expert depth itself contributing to perceived and actual value.
4. **Meaningful growth:** Symbolic systems inspire hypotheses for self-reflection, not rigid declarations about someone's nature or fate. Users shape their experience through voluntary reflection, choose aspirations, practice skills, and record actual milestones. Every insight can lead to **Show Me Why** and **Show Me How**.
5. **Affordable agency:** Social participation is entirely free; useful digital features have a generous free layer, affordable one-time purchases, and optional all-access subscription. Users can earn premium functionality through legitimate contributions or obtain sponsor-supported access. No hidden or fear-based upsells.
6. **Positive and humane:** Gas users up with credible, constructive possibility, not doom astrology. Build a more loving, encouraging, empowered community, including thoughtful design for younger audiences. No shame, spiritual rankings, exploitative predictions, or negative fixed labels.
7. **Release quality = “13/10”:** No dead buttons, broken routes, fake production claims, awkwardly cropped people, sluggish transitions, illegible text, inconsistent components, or unfinished core journeys. Inspect rendered screens and functional behavior before release; private prototyping is not the public launch.

**Key principle:** **“Depth from day one. Discovery at your own pace.”**

## 3. Mission, ethos, and historical/scientific integrity

### Mission in brief
Make self-discovery beautiful, personal, empowering, and accessible by combining historical/symbolic traditions, modern technology, and practical growth guidance. Foster an inclusive community where people's growth, kindness, creativity, and achievements help encourage one another.

### Five principles

- **Illuminate, never intimidate:** No frightening cosmic predictions, curses, inevitable breakup/failure statements, or selling remedies for invented spiritual dangers.
- **Celebrate potential without counterfeit certainty:** Encourage constructive possibilities without pretending astrology or numerology objectively diagnoses personality, predicts outcomes, or guarantees talent.
- **Reward growth, not superiority:** Don't create public leaderboards ranking spiritual worth or status. Cosmetics can signal paid membership without asserting paid people are better humans.
- **Make kindness contagious:** Encourage support, celebration, collaboration, mutual inspiration, and positive social interactions.
- **Keep opportunity open:** Financial limitations must not bar all self-discovery. Allow free participation and honest, attainable routes to earned access.

### Grounding
Actual planetary position mathematics can be verified astronomically; traditional astrological and numerological meanings are **symbolic interpretations**, not scientifically established measurements of identity or destiny. Separate calculation facts, interpretive traditions, user-confirmed lived experience, and practical guidance. **No AI-fabricated placements or false calculation provenance.** Distinguish historically grounded alchemy (which also included practical material experimentation) from later inner/psychological readings, particularly Jungian interpretation. Avoid implying a single four-stage formulation was universal across all historical alchemy.

### Age-sensitive design
Age-appropriate experiences, stronger privacy and consent for minors, restrictions on discoverability/direct messaging as appropriate, no pressuring younger members into monetized referrals or status spending, no harmful fixed identity labels.

## 4. Creative direction: what the site MUST look and feel like

### Source imagery to attach to the builder
The founder selected the **original mystical purple-and-gold celestial website concept** as the central visual direction because it was more spacious, magical, and less cluttered than the futuristic blue design. They also loved the **luminous constellation-filled human figure from the futuristic blue design**, which should replace the original purple concept's traveler/girl. A refined purple composite exists showing that combination. Attach these specific generated reference images to the builder as primary art-direction anchors. Additional founder-selected reference imagery includes: dense-but-readable black starfields; grey/misty nebula clouds; celestial golden geometry; radiant golden lotus motifs; astral wheels, linked constellations, spiraling pathways, an alchemical laboratory, star-linked human silhouettes, and a purple/gold Home Galaxy.

**IMPORTANT:** The reference images are inspiration/source assets, not permission to screenshot the website and use a flat UI image as a clickable page. Build real, accessible interface controls and real responsive compositions. Any external reference imagery must have appropriate usage rights before public release.

### Visual language

- **Environment:** Near-black space and deep midnight-purple/violet/indigo regions, with actual visual depth: carefully layered nebulae, celestial clouds, distant galaxies, celestial architecture/portals, refined sacred geometry, elegant glow, planetary forms, and stardust.
- **Stars:** Neither a sparse scattering nor an overbearing blizzard. Most tiny and restrained; some brighter; a minority emit subtle violet glows. Cloud/mist formations add dimension. The whole scene must not degrade into a generic wallpaper.
- **Accent lighting:** Warm champagne/antique gold, occasionally iridescent celestial blue; gold orbital paths, celestial motifs, and ornamental geometry. Typography primarily warm ivory with restrained gold highlights.
- **Typography:** Luxurious editorial serif display headings paired with highly legible modern sans-serif UI labels/body. No tiny, barely visible body copy on a large desktop screen.
- **Components:** Beautiful, restrained translucent dark-violet frosted glass with subtle backdrop blur, warm-gold edge illumination, tasteful dimensional shadows, luminous hover/focus states. Avoid generic neon-glass UI kits or clutter.
- **Lines/dividers:** Slightly stronger **warm-gold** dividers (not razor-thin grey). Where necessary aim around 1.5–2 visually effective CSS pixels with subtle gradient/illumination; consistent reusable tokens.
- **Layout:** Premium cinematic composition, considered negative space, excellent hierarchy. Not empty minimalist editorial pages, but also not a cluttered space-themed dashboard. Spatial, cohesive, intentional, readable, genuinely responsive (phone/tablet/desktop/ultrawide).
- **Character:** Serene, elegant glowing human silhouette/body filled with stars/constellation geometry, with selected avatar variants. **Never awkwardly crop or cut off the figure's head or face.** Use dedicated responsive crop/composition, not one desktop art image forcibly stretched into mobile.
- **Movement:** Cinematic when it matters (arrival, discoveries, unlocking worlds, meaningful progress); subtle when reading. Optimized, not gratuitously animated.

### What the initial prototype got wrong
Screenshots showed the website had drifted into an **under-scaled conventional dark editorial site**: tiny text, sparse cosmic wallpaper, small plain progress diagrams, weak grey hairline dividers, ordinary controls, misplaced/uncentered footer tagline, badly framed/cropped celestial character, and relatively little of the reference art. The founder specifically said it did **not resemble the original purple reference** and did not feel cinematic/interactive. The user later saw richer nebula art on the Journey page, which was progress, but the galaxy remained background wallpaper and the active interactive experience was still small and conventional. A previous screenshot annotation flagged persistent bottom-row vertical alignment errors, unimplemented frosted-glass CTA/info panels, thin grey lines, and footer-center misalignment.

**Do not treat minor CSS tweaks as a full art-direction fix.** Restore the original heroic composition and premium world design, then make interactions cinematic, not just their backdrop.

## 6. Signature arrival: “returning to your universe”

The founder explicitly described the animation:

1. Entry/sign-in screen: dark, cloudy, softly starry sky; tasteful login controls; memorable “Come Home” / “Return Home” copy.
2. After a successful auth action (or explicit demo simulation): a brief celestial sound/whoosh, when sound is allowed.
3. Clouds pull into a **short spiral in two opposing directions**, opening to reveal the previously hidden vibrant glowing personalized galaxy, not a standard route fade.
4. The reveal blooms with golden light, visual depth, and particles. The user's constellation avatar resolves at the central **Celestial Soul Hub**.
5. An elegant welcome (“Welcome home,” etc.), then the user immediately has usable navigational control.
6. On the very first arrival, a short introductory overlay/guide appears. Underlying galaxy interaction is visually subdued/disabled **only while that onboarding overlay is active**. The guide includes a tick box to remember preference to **show or skip future cinematic arrivals**. Explicitly save the selection. Future visits honor it.
7. Controls for replaying introduction, skipping animation, muting sound, reduced motion, keyboard navigation, and faster direct entry. Never require audio autoplay, block login on an unnecessary animation, or force users to repeat onboarding.
8. Once the entry animation ends, interior routes must not display a floating “Skip to experience” control when nothing is playing (observed old defect).

This should be a signature memory-making moment; polish and performance are equally necessary.

## 7. The Celestial Soul Hub / Home Galaxy is the unifying product metaphor

**A single galaxy used three ways:**

1. **Owner dashboard:** Navigable, personal, alive. Visit planets/orbs/constellations/portals to access product destinations; glimpse recent milestones, growth paths, current cycles, friends, and rewards. The first user sees meaningful ways to explore, not fake locked filler.
2. **Social profile and cover/banner:** The galaxy becomes a recognizable expression of the user's identity; their standard avatar also appears beside forum posts, comments, messages, and testimonials.
3. **Visitor experience:** Friends and optionally guests can admire permitted portions of a user's universe, selected achievements and public placements. Its URL can be shared off-platform as an invitation to create one's own galaxy.

**Owner vs visitor data access is fundamentally different.** Never expose private birth details, quiz responses, journals, or private pathways merely because someone is a friend. Discovery and sharing are opt-in and granular.

### World design

- Spatial, interconnected environments, not just a feature grid. Each destination should feel like somewhere: observatory, numerology realm, growth constellations, connection bridge, celestial time/timing object, Starforge, distant laboratory.
- Navigable on desktop (pointer/focus), mobile (touch/swipe), plus conventional clear menus, accessible shortcuts, search if useful, and always a reliable **Return to Celestial Soul Hub** option.
- Regions with notifications or new activity pulse/glow subtly, without sensory overload. Friends' achievements, a fresh transit reading, a ready growth step, new rewards, etc. can affect the environment.
- **New features** should appear on a user's first subsequent visit as beautiful newly illuminated celestial objects; curiosity draws people to inspect them. Don't fabricate new features or use misleading availability.
- Progress unlocks and milestones illuminate constellations and visually transform objects. Progress is meaningful for all tiers, not contingent on purchase.
- The site feels like a navigable cosmos. The galaxy must not remain inert wallpaper behind generic cards.

### Progressive reveal (the WoW analogy)

The founder explicitly prefers the pacing of the **first levels of World of Warcraft**: users see an intriguing, extensive world, but are introduced to complexity step by step and become fluent with basics before more advanced systems. Some mysterious destinations visible in the distance invite curiosity. Never make all feature doors invisible, but avoid a chaotic initial menu. **Depth from day one, discovery at your own pace.**

### Locks / access paths

- **Open:** Immediately available.
- **Discoverable:** Unlock through clear, relevant introductory exploration or optionally purchase immediate access where the product permits.
- **Premium:** Requires appropriate separately purchased module, active Pro entitlement, or Stardust-earned functional access as applicable.
- **Coming Soon:** Explicitly labeled not yet available; no fake working controls or deceptive promises.

Important: **someone who already purchased a module cannot be forced through unrelated progression to use it.** If a free progression path promises full access, it must genuinely unlock the promised thing and remain available under stated terms. When a feature is locked, explain the relevant steps or price up front and allow browsing/returning to the galaxy.

**Example copy:** “The Alchemical Laboratory — Where the mundane becomes magnificent. Explore your universe to awaken this destination, or purchase the complete experience for immediate access.”

## 8. Meaningful module architecture (substance and cohesion, not feature count theater)

### 8.1 The Observatory — Astrology

- Deterministic ephemeris-backed chart calculation using date, time, historical timezone and place. Handle unknown birth time explicitly; do not fabricate Rising/house certainty.
- Approachable visual chart; click planet, house, aspect to highlight only relevant connections and explain them. Adjustable complexity and aspect filters.
- **Discover:** Plain-English Big Three, chart ruler, strongest patterns; beginner-friendly overview.
- **Explore:** Layered wheel, more relationships, expanded interpretations and glossaries.
- **Expert:** Traditional detailed chart wheel, glyphs/degrees, aspect tables, configurable orbs, house systems (e.g., Placidus and Whole Sign), Tropical/Sidereal options where accurately implemented, possibly asteroid toggles. These tools included in the corresponding entitlement rather than splintered upsells.
- Calculations and user-facing interpretations must reference the same authoritative data; technical toggles should show their effects clearly and not silently alter interpretations.

### 8.2 The Numerology Chamber

- Life Path from DOB, Expression and Soul Urge from name when supplied with appropriate consent, method and details explained. Never claim exact value without actually calculating it.
- Complete, readable symbolic explanations; personalized cycles (Year/Month/Day) where implemented.
- Connect numerology with astrology cautiously, showing apparent complementary themes or tensions without claiming empirical proof.

### 8.3 Your Blueprint — synthesis, not cookbook fragments

- Start with a coherent, relevant, concise narrative prioritizing major patterns: Sun/Moon/Rising, chart ruler, tight aspects, stelliums, other important configurations, plus key numerological factors.
- Rather than a long generic list, identify highest-salience themes and their possible expression in everyday life, framed as interpretive possibilities.
- Each insight offers **Show Me Why**, **Does This Resonate?**, **Show Me How**, **Save**, and **Explore Further** where relevant.
- Good experience: perhaps 3 substantial, relevant insights up front, with broader library always available.

### 8.4 Show Me Why — evidence/interpretation transparency

- Reveal *which* verified chart placements, relationships, and number calculations led to an interpretation.
- Distinguish mathematical inputs from traditional symbolic reading and AI explanatory synthesis.
- Link visually into appropriate chart location and professional details.

### 8.5 Resonance Engine — personalized reflection

- Short, optional, beautiful prompts after chart/numerology generation asking which suggestions match lived experiences. Include “not me,” “sometimes,” uncertainty, and nuanced/non-binary possibilities. No pseudo-scientific truth verdict.
- Track FOUR different concepts separately: **symbolic blueprint**, **reported resonance**, **user aspirations**, **observed real-world progress**.
- Use these to prioritize the content/pathways shown next. **Personalization changes priority, never permission.** All paths and interpretations remain accessible in Explore Everything/library.
- Someone who doesn't identify as a leader but wants to develop leadership must be encouraged to try; never lock them into a label.
- Answers can be edited/revisited/deleted; users may change over time. Quizzes should never be a required gate or a proof someone matches a chart.

### 8.6 Show Me How — from symbolic insight to action

- Concrete growth experiments, realistic achievable steps, examples of potential talents/interests as *invitations to explore*, not guaranteed gifts implied by birth date.
- Example: one idea becomes a seven-day creative challenge; from noticing strengths -> experimenting -> practice -> reflecting -> adjusting goals.
- User selects paths such as creativity, leadership, confidence, emotional balance, communication, self-trust, relationships, purpose, focus, contribution. This is not a fixed destiny algorithm.
- Progress evolves from user feedback and actual actions rather than astrology alone.

### 8.7 Your Journey / The Pathways

- Branching **interactive constellation** is the focal point, not a five-tiny-dots line chart pasted over nebula wallpaper.
- Progress stages such as Discover, Activate, Practice, Integrate, Embody. Distinguish completed, selected, available, undiscovered nodes; readable descriptions and practical next actions.
- Selecting a node reveals elegant information; completing one sends a tasteful gold illumination pulse through a connected pathway, updates progress and can unlock a celestial decoration. Reduced-motion alternative.
- Reflect and adapt; no shame streaks or comparative spiritual rankings; users choose their own relevant goals.

### 8.8 The Celestial Clock / Your Cycles

- Interactive transit timeline and relevant numerological personal cycles. Allow date scrolling; coherent explanations tied to their own natal data when available.
- Avoid absolute claims about “best days” or harmful predictions. Reflective suggestions can be offered with uncertainty and user agency.

### 8.9 Connected Universes — social (free core feature)

- Optional discovery, selectively shared placements (Sun, Moon, Rising, Life Path), mutual friends, optional visitor galaxy view, carefully opted-in milestone activity, notifications, encouragement/reactions, and shared growth activities.
- Share achievements and “I found my calling” experiences through beautiful, automatically generated branded share cards, caption and deep links; use native share on devices when possible, download/copy fallback.
- Linked worlds/connection constellations illustrate friendships and positive growth, but public/private settings control what is visible.
- No paid gate to core friend discovery/connection, profile access, community, basic sharing, or shared accomplishments.
- No arbitrary user-uploaded profile photos: curated celestial avatars keep the art cohesive and reduce image moderation burden (text and behavior moderation still required).

### 8.10 Starforge — galaxies and celestial identity

- A curated Sims-like design studio: selectable constellation patterns, star colors/types/density, particle/stardust effects, nebula colors, lighting, planets/moons/rings, celestial ornaments, atmospheric themes.
- Carefully constrained presets, placements and options preserve art direction and prevent offensive drawings/symbol construction. Don't allow arbitrary uploads or fully unconstrained drawing; moderation/reporting still needed for user-provided names/text.
- Galaxy is also a public-profile cover and owner dashboard; changes should not break location functionality or performance.
- Tiers specified in section 9.

### 8.11 The Alchemical Laboratory / The Inner Alchemist

- A captivating, historically informed personal growth path using a well-known four-color symbolic interpretation of alchemical transformation:
  - **Nigredo / Blackening:** Letting old patterns break down; confronting beliefs constructively, no shaming.
  - **Albedo / Whitening:** Clarity, reflection, separating assumptions from evidence.
  - **Citrinitas / Yellowing:** Inspiration, integration and real-world experiments.
  - **Rubedo / Reddening:** Embodiment, wholeness as an ongoing practice rather than final perfection.
- Intro available visibly within galaxy; deeper experience can be legitimately discoverable/paid as advertised. Design a mysterious but welcoming distant laboratory with a visually transforming orb/stone, culminating in a displayable galaxy decoration. Repeatable focused journeys rather than “attain enlightenment forever.”
- Explain the difference between historical material alchemy and later symbolism/psychology. No dangerous chemical instruction and no supernatural certainty.

### 8.12 Future specialist modules / integrations

- Potential additional frameworks: Human Design, Gene Keys, guided journaling, more sophisticated compatibility, AI Ask Your Chart, digital keepsake exports, audio, live practitioners, partner offers. **Do not claim these are implemented.** Human Design/Gene Keys may have intellectual property/licensing concerns, requiring review before commercial implementation.

**Every launch destination should have:** meaningful content, a substantive interaction, a next step, a way home, working navigation, and clear demo/production status. Never ship a decorated empty page for perceived breadth.

## 9. Social identity and cosmetic tier system

This is a deliberate purchase conversion mechanism, like a sophisticated cosmetic status layer in gaming/Discord Nitro, **without restricting the free social network or implying spiritual superiority**.

| Entitlement | Free / Soulseed | Qualifying one-time buyer / Astral Bloom | Active paid Pro / Starlight Aura |

## 10. Purchase architecture, access and rewards

### Three commercial routes, not forced subscription

1. **Free forever:** Meaningful interactive starting blueprint, numerology basics, selected insights, introductory path, free social networking, and the ability to explore many modules to a substantive introductory depth.
2. **Affordable one-time ownership:** Buy a specific complete static report/module and **keep it forever**. Example *test* prices: natal blueprint $4.99; numerology $2.99; strengths/potential $3.99; compatibility $4.99; 30-day dated forecast $2.99 for that period; bundle $12.99. Entry items can start around $1.99. Don't upsell every planet/aspect separately; complete purchases must truly be complete within their stated scope.
3. **Pro subscription:** Working hypothesis about $9.99/month for full ongoing digital experience, expanded compute-dependent services (fair-use limits), advanced capabilities, and paid Pro-exclusive cosmetics. No Spirit Guide human session included by default.

**Prices are hypotheses, not validated commercial rates.** Transaction fees can matter on $1.99 items; bundle where useful, avoid duplicate purchases, prorate/credit upgrades fairly, show clear permanent ownership versus time-bound features, easy cancellation, no surprise subscription activation.

### 30-day competitor “Cosmic Upgrade” / Discovery Pass

A launch promotion intended to attract already-paying astrology consumers from an approved small list of recognizable providers, e.g., CHANI, The Pattern, Co–Star, Nebula, TimePassages (**final eligibility and provider offering validation pending**).

**Latest confirmed decision:** Not 30 days of unrestricted Pro. It's a **30-day Discovery Pass**: deeper-than-free but clearly limited previews across a range of modules, enough for meaningful excitement, with deeper features reserved for purchases or Pro. No exclusive paid subscriber avatar/profile frame. No card or auto-renewal required. Do not deceptively label as “full Pro” if it isn't.

Qualifying purchases only, one per person, small pilot (example 500), original email purchase confirmation where possible instead of screenshot-only approvals, sender authentication and duplicate detection, manual review for ambiguous cases. Third parties generally cannot fully validate unrelated vendors' in-app purchases; no false claim of foolproof verification, no credentials requested, data minimization and deletion. Allow redacted private data. Seek feedback about providers tried and dissatisfaction.

### Stardust: Earn Your Universe

- Rewards for **verified value**, especially genuine new active members, paid conversions, activated referrals, approved Spirit Guide referrals, and legitimately attributed shared milestone acquisition.
- No pay merely for posting links/clicks. No spam, bot farming, fake accounts, self-referrals, or false testimonials. Pending/settled reward validation and anti-fraud.
- No developer bug-report bounties or expert-authored educational resources in the mainstream consumer earning list: founder explicitly rejected those as misaligned with everyday/younger audiences.
- A path to substantial digital-feature access for those unable to afford subscriptions; sponsor-funded access for people without networks or marketing reach. Suggested credits and costs are preliminary, not fixed.
- **Functional entitlements through credits** can be full within stated quotas, but paid-Pro exclusive cosmetic identity may remain exclusive to cash subscribers.
- Referral bonuses/endorsements disclosed accurately under applicable advertising rules; no incentives conditional on positive reviews. Stronger protections or no commercial referral incentives for minors.

### Spirit Guides — later marketplace

- Independent astrologers/numerologists/intuitive practitioners/growth specialists can offer sessions or carefully described personalized reports; curated profiles and transparent services.
- Proposed commission **20%** (e.g., $10 platform revenue on a $50 session), net of costs/other arrangements. Guides organically promote their own on-site storefronts, attracting both paid customers and free users.
- Payment routing marketplace infrastructure, provider verification, clear refunds/disputes, truthful claims, opt-in sharing of relevant chart details. Core interpretation engine never steers people to a paid guide with fear.

### Sponsors over ads

- **No invasive ads** anywhere in the emotional journey: no irrelevant popups, intrusive banners, interstitials, attention hijacking, or personal-data monetization.
- Curated mission-aligned sponsors recognized tastefully in footer and optional supporters page, perhaps **“If You Love Us, You Might Love Them”**, with sponsorship disclosure and truthful partner information.
- Sponsor-supported premium access programs can subsidize free users without subjecting them to ads.
- Sponsors must never influence reading content or exploit private birth/quiz/journal data.

## 12. Privacy, safety, and operational integrity

- Precise birth date, time, location; personal reflections; journaling; survey answers; private goals are **private by default**. Profile discovery, visible Sun/Moon/Rising/Life Path, relationships, milestones, and map sharing are separate opt-in choices.
- Friend acceptance doesn't automatically expose sensitive data. Profile visitors see only explicitly shared information.
- Only first-party curated avatar designs rather than arbitrary uploaded profile photographs. Still moderate usernames, comments, posts, harassment, fraud, and abuse.
- Safer experience for minors; default conservative social privacy, avoid exposing location or sensitive attributes, appropriate age constraints and parental legal requirements.
- Ethical AI: no deterministic “you are broken” predictions, harmful advice, fabricated astrological calculations, or clinician replacement claims. Individual experience directs growth pathways.
- No intrusive ads or sponsor manipulation. No exploitative fear-based paywalls, deliberately frustrating trials, misleading timers, or false scarcity.
- Payments and sessions: real entitlement enforcement, transparent billing, fraud checks, refund/cancellation flow and secure data storage before public release.

## 13. Engineering and interaction expectations

- A coherent shared design system and reusable components; CSS/layout tokens; avoid isolated one-off visual patches that regress elsewhere.
- Accessible browser experience, touchscreen and keyboard navigation, visible focus states, high contrast body copy and controls, semantic elements, reduced motion, sound off by default or controlled by user interaction, fast direct-entry fallback.
- Responsive art-directed compositions rather than mobile shrinking of a desktop collage; test phones, tablets, desktop and ultrawide. Protect head/face composition at every breakpoint.
- Motion optimized and purposeful: lightweight CSS/SVG/canvas or GPU-aware effects; lazy load scenes, control particle counts, avoid continuous costly effects, respect battery/data usage and poor devices.
- Avoid decorative buttons that do nothing. Distinguish valid prototypes (real clicks with demo data) from production readiness (live ephemeris, persistent accounts, trusted social, payment processing).
- Accurate deterministic charts; real time-zone and daylight-saving logic, licensing check for ephemeris, unknown birth-time pathways. Do not silently claim compatibility of frameworks or exact house/aspect data.
- Provide meaningful empty/loading/error/offline states and escape routes; no dead ends, no 404s, robust URL routing and refresh.
