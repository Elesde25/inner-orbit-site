## Full-edition additions and remaining gaps
- **Tao Te Ching:** the 81 chapters of James Legge's 1891 translation were imported from the Standard Ebooks source transcription at https://github.com/standardebooks/laozi_tao-te-ching_james-legge/blob/master/src/epub/text/tao-te-ching.xhtml. Notes and introductory material are not included. This is a historical public-domain translation in the United States; check local jurisdiction and do a line-by-line editorial review. Earlier curated readings are retained separately to preserve existing annotations.
- **Dhammapada:** the 26 chapters, covering verse numbers 1–423, are based on F. Max Müller's 1881 translation (Project Gutenberg #2017) from https://github.com/GITenberg/Dhammapada-a-Collection-of-Verses--Being-One-of-the-Canonical-Books-of-the-Buddhists_2017/blob/master/2017.txt. Nine passages combine adjacent verse numbers in the transcription. The Gutenberg transcription omits verse 127 and labels the text of verse 128 as 127; verse 127 was restored and the numbering corrected against https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/Dhammapada_(Muller). Introductory material, footnotes and index are excluded. The numbering and source coverage were checked automatically; a full line-by-line review against an 1881 scan remains necessary. Earlier curated readings remain available for existing annotations.
- **Not all texts are complete.** Gita, Meditations, Enchiridion, Ecclesiastes and Varieties remain curated selections or original overviews, and some original books remain unfinished. No newly invented text has been represented as a complete historical edition. Complete versions require edition-by-edition source, rights, editorial and transcription work; original unfinished volumes require supplied manuscripts or an explicit decision to commission newly written material.
- Original rendered cover images are retained for thumbnails; derived front-only crops remove photographic spines and fore-edges on inspectable Three.js books. These are crops of existing supplied/generated imagery, not new source artwork.
