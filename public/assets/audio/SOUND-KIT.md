# Inner Orbit original effects — IO-004, version 1

These three effects are generated entirely by the repository's `scripts/make-sound-kit.py`, using deterministic oscillators, envelopes and seeded noise. No external recording, sampled song, purchased library, voice, or competitor asset is used. They were created specifically for Inner Orbit; no third-party sample licence is required for these generated source materials. This records provenance, not a guarantee of copyright protection or exclusive ownership of simple tones.

- `realm-travel.wav`: 1.65-second airy stellar-current whoosh.
- `gateway-arrival.wav`: 1.90-second gentle three-note arrival.
- `milestone-light.wav`: 2.20-second ascending illumination reward.

Format: 24 kHz mono, 16-bit PCM WAV. Consistent RMS target with a -9 dBFS peak ceiling, click-free fade edges, no looping. Playback uses the existing independent effects channel and saved user volume; cues replace one another rather than stacking.

The existing `inner-orbit-celestial.mp3` is unchanged and is not covered by this new-effects provenance statement. The full kit still needs environmental music layers, selection/glimmer/Starforge effects, music-rights review, and human listening/mix approval on actual devices. No YouTube audio was copied.
