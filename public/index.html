YOUR SPACE IS YOURS

Privacy, by design.

Most universe features are browser-local experiences. Soul Journal uses a server-backed account. Social profiles, rewards and access tiers use clearly labeled demonstration behavior. Feature-specific explanations identify calculated results, symbolic readings and local storage.

Progress, optional reflections, resonance responses, aspirations, sharing choices, cosmetic previews and experience preferences stay in this browser by default. If you sign in and enable a category in Library account settings, selected data also syncs to your account. Turning a category off removes its synced copy and keeps this browser’s local files. Numerology is calculated locally from supplied inputs. Only choosing Save this reading stores its name, optional birth date and exploration date in this browser. Clear inputs & saved reading removes that numerology copy. Soul Journal accounts use your email and a hashed password. Reflections you explicitly save are stored on the server under your account, including the question, response, chart context and date. Sign out on shared devices. Clearing browser progress does not delete account journal entries; use Delete reflection in your journal. There are no real payments or live social connections.
